Principles

How I think about product

01

Start with the underlying problem

Understand the workflow, incentives and constraints before proposing a solution.

02

Make ownership and trade-offs explicit

Clarify what I owned, what the team decided together and what engineering implemented.

03

Measure outcomes, not activity

Tie decisions to adoption, efficiency, quality or commercial outcomes.
