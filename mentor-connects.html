01

The Problem

Mentor profiles, webinar schedules and registration information often become separate operational records. This makes it difficult to maintain accurate mentor information and understand programme activity from one place.

The challenge was not simply storing mentor and webinar data. It was connecting mentor onboarding, webinar planning, registrations and administrative oversight into one manageable workflow.

Product Goal and Users

Create a focused platform where mentors can complete their professional profiles and manage webinar activity, while administrators can oversee mentors, sessions and registrations without switching between disconnected tools.

  • Review all webinars
  • View platform-wide schedules

MVP Scope and Non-Goals

To deliver a working solution within a short development cycle, we defined a strict MVP scope and deferred advanced integrations to later phases.

MVP Scope (In-Goal)

  • Google Sign-up/Login verification
  • Multi-step mentor onboarding flow
  • Webinar creation and scheduling
  • Composio calendar synchronization
  • Admin approval & registration tracking

Deferred Features (Non-Goals)

  • Payment Integrations: Paid webinars or ticket purchases are deferred; all sessions are free.
  • Advanced Analytics: Participant engagement rates and drop-off reports are deferred; only raw registration counts are shown.
  • Automated Reminders: Automated notification loops are deferred (relying on manual calendar notifications).

Key Product Decisions

Decision 01

Optional LinkedIn import with manual fallback

LinkedIn import reduces the effort required to create a professional mentor profile, while manual entry ensures onboarding can continue when the external integration is unavailable or incomplete.

Decision 02

Separate mentor and administrator experiences

Mentors need a focused workspace for profile and webinar management. Administrators need cross-platform visibility across mentors, sessions and onboarding status.

Decision 03

Connecting mentor profiles with webinar history

Keeping professional profile information and session history together helps administrators understand both who a mentor is and how they are participating in the platform.

Decision 04

Supporting both list and calendar views

The list view supports detailed session management, while the calendar view provides a faster understanding of scheduling conflicts and upcoming activity.

04

Mentor Onboarding

Mentors begin by signing up and choosing how to complete their profile. The platform integrates with LinkedIn via Composio OAuth, pulling in the mentor's name, headline, bio and work history automatically. Mentors who prefer not to import — or whose import fails — complete the same form manually without any disruption to the flow.

After importing or entering their details, mentors review a preview of their mentor card before completing setup. Profile completion is required before a mentor can access their dashboard or create webinars.

Profile preview — mentors review their card before completing onboarding

05

Webinar Management

After completing onboarding, mentors access a dashboard that shows total webinars, enrolment totals and upcoming sessions at a glance. From the Webinars page, they can switch between a list view and a calendar view.

Mentor dashboard — upcoming sessions and activity summary

The create-webinar modal collects the webinar title, description, date, time, target audience and meeting link. Form inputs persist across page reloads using session storage, so a draft is not lost if the mentor navigates away. Once published, the webinar appears in the mentor's list, and mentors can click through to see the registrations associated with each session.

06

Administrative Oversight

Administrators access a separate portal with platform-wide visibility. The Mentor Management page lists all registered mentors with their onboarding status and allows search by name, university or company. Clicking through to a mentor profile shows their full webinar history.

A FullCalendar-powered calendar gives administrators a cross-platform view of all webinar sessions. Each mentor's sessions are colour-coded using a deterministic hash, making it easy to distinguish whose sessions fall when at a glance.

Global Calendar — FullCalendar with per-mentor colour coding

Learnings and Product Gallery

Learning 01

Automation needs a fallback

External integrations should reduce effort without becoming a single point of failure. The LinkedIn import is the clearest example: useful when it works, non-blocking when it doesn't.

Learning 02

Role-based products need genuinely different experiences

Mentors and administrators share the same underlying data but need different permissions, views and actions. Designing for both without blending the two was a deliberate product constraint.

Learning 03

Product and implementation decisions shape one another

Building end-to-end required balancing ideal workflows with authentication, data, integration and interface constraints. Owning the full stack forces you to understand why your product decisions are hard to implement — and to find the right trade-offs rather than pushing them to someone else.
