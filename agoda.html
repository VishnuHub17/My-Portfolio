Case Study

Agoda — Booking Assurance

An independent product case study reframing a booking-trust problem into a prioritized, metric-driven solution.

Independent Case Study TravelTech Marketplace Trust
Independent case study · Not affiliated with Agoda · Metrics are proposed, not actual results
The Problem

Agoda users get platform confirmation but lack visibility into whether the hotel has received, acknowledged, and matched their reservation. That gap between "platform says confirmed" and "hotel actually has your booking" is where real trust breaks down — and where operational failures hide until check-in day.

Evidence base: public regulatory reports (Japan Tourism Agency), hotel-side warnings (VMG Hotels), and Agoda partner documentation.

Why I Made This Call

Started with cancellation and refund friction from my own booking experience. Then realised the more defensible, systemic problem was the trust gap between platform confirmation and hotel acknowledgment — not just a missing cancel button. Reframed the entire case study around Booking Assurance instead of a cancellation flow. The cancel button is a symptom; the trust gap is the disease.

Proposed Solution: Booking Assurance Layer
  • Hotel acknowledgment status (has the hotel confirmed receipt?)
  • Booking detail match check (do the hotel's records match what you booked?)
  • Hotel reference number (surfaced to the traveler for direct verification)
  • Verification timeline (when to expect acknowledgment)
  • Risk detection (flags high-risk bookings proactively)
  • Issue tracker (visible trail if something goes wrong pre-arrival)
Artifacts

Proposed Success Metrics
Hotel acknowledgment completion rate
Time to acknowledgment (target window)
Booking detail match rate
Pre-trip support ticket volume (target: reduce)
Booking confidence score (target: increase)
Retrospect

The main learning: separating user-facing promises from operational system states is where the hardest PM work lives. "Confirmed" on the UI means nothing if the system behind it hasn't closed the loop with the hotel.
